Finale 97 (internal version 3.8), uncompressed fixed-row era, big-endian.

Origin: test cases authored for the Settings Scrapbook plug-in and released for
publication by their author.

F97-fileinfo-short.mus
    sha256 35b764881cf54f67e2e4dfcd3d1f7071a330fd03e0f7a5afb6314a6b4857eb29
    A document whose file-info text fits inside the usual header, so its record
    body begins at the customary offset 0x200.

F97-fileinfo-long.mus
    sha256 01c845fbcdfc27a4f61033f66dbd16bf3a72a2bde6ff4cc4dda46733389ebf03
    The same document with deliberately long file-info text. The text overruns
    0x200 and the record body begins at 0x1167 instead. The header records that
    offset at 0x60 as a byte-order-sensitive 16-bit value.

The pair is a controlled difference: they vary only in the length of the
file-info strings, which makes them the evidence that the record body offset is
a header field rather than the constant 0x200.

Fin97-baseline.mus / .etf and Fin97-clef-baseline.mus / .etf
    Finale 97, internally Enigma version 3.8.0. The clef-baseline save changes two
    clefs' baseline adjustments: selector 28 word 4 moves 0 -> 1 and selector 29
    word 4 moves 0 -> -2, and nothing else in the record set changes.
    Finale 97 has no "use baseline adjustments" checkbox and adjusts
    unconditionally, so its companion converts both values at 768 Efix per harmonic
    level even though word 5 of selector 28 is 30, whose bit 0 is clear. This pair
    is what shows the switch is version-gated rather than universal.
