Finale 2.6.3, classic Mac OS.
    Mac OS 9.0.4 running in SheepShaver, which is the host for every Finale before
    2004. The exact application build is not recorded.

F263-baseline.mus
    12,848 bytes. The unedited document the other F263 fixtures derive from. Its
    nine ordered `HS`/`HT` pairs produce nine source TextBlocks. `HS` word 5 low
    values 0, 1, and 2 correspond to left, right, and center justification in the
    Finale 27 companion. Finale inserts and renumbers other TextBlocks during the
    upgrade, but semantic text plus layout identifies all nine source blocks.

F263-music-font.mus
    12,848 bytes. Changes the default music font. See ../../../research/FORMAT_NOTES.md.

F263-clef-baseline.mus
    sha256 685fbeb932be3b503775a4f5749a6dad456f3fde525fbeeacf297b33245d81d4
    12,848 bytes. Changes the Clef Designer baseline adjustment on two clefs and
    nothing else. It differs from F263-baseline.mus in exactly three bytes:
    selector `28(65534)` word 4 moves -2 -> 1 and `29(65534)` word 4 moves -4 -> -2.
    The values are harmonic levels. Finale 27 discards them, emitting no
    `<baseAdjust>` in the companion.

F263-clef-baseline.etf
    sha256 0a54adf5445503e1727b6c566f0de43addebcf896121aa408ece7a2140392252
    A Finale 2.6.3 ETF export of the same document, carrying the era's own
    `ENIGMA Structures Copyright 1987 by Coda.` banner. Its `^28(65534)` and
    `^29(65534)` rows confirm the edited words, and `^24(65534) 13 69 52 48 65 60`
    confirms from a second era that selector 24 is not the default-font array
    before Finale 2002.

Finale 27 companions are in `-finale27/`.

F263-courtesy-key-off.mus / .etf
    Only the courtesy key signature turned off. The change is selector 12 word 1,
    1 -> 0, not the packed courtesy word selector 44 uses from Finale 3.0 onward:
    the Coda era stores the three courtesies as separate boolean words. Which word
    holds the clef's is still open, so the reader leaves cautionaryClefChanges at the
    Finale 27 default for this era.

F263-dateform-tab.mus
    sha256 f87de69e8e79ea9a15d222a3e18c26894d2d702462b5fedf16ec490986807016
    From F263-baseline, the date format set to Abbreviated and the tab stop to 7 spaces.
    Selector 05 word 5 moves 0 -> 2 and selector 13 word 0 moves 4 -> 7, against a companion
    reading <dateFormat>abbrev</dateFormat>. The value distinguishes the third DateFormat
    member from the second, which the Finale 1.0.0 save fixes.
