F263-baseline.mus
    12,848 bytes. The unedited document the other F263 fixtures derive from. Its
    nine ordered `HS`/`HT` pairs produce nine source TextBlocks. `HS` word 5 low
    values 0, 1, and 2 correspond to left, right, and center justification in the
    Finale 27 companion. Finale inserts and renumbers other TextBlocks during the
    upgrade, but semantic text plus layout identifies all nine source blocks.

F263-music-font.mus

F263-clef-baseline.mus
    sha256 685fbeb932be3b503775a4f5749a6dad456f3fde525fbeeacf297b33245d81d4
    12,848 bytes. Changes the Clef Designer baseline adjustment on two clefs and
    nothing else. It differs from F263-baseline.mus in exactly three bytes:
    selector `28(65534)` word 4 moves -2 -> 1 and `29(65534)` word 4 moves -4 -> -2.
    The values are harmonic levels. Finale 27 discards them, emitting no
    `<baseAdjust>` in the companion.
