Finale 1.0.0 for Macintosh, Coda-banner era, big-endian.

Origin: controlled test documents created by Robert G. Patterson in Finale
1.0.0 running under Mac OS 9 hosted by SheepShaver. The documents and their
musical content are released by their author under this repository's MIT
license.

All three changed files were derived from the same newly created baseline. Each
changes one default-font control. Pairwise comparison among the changed files
localizes the intended changes to the named physical tuples; the baseline has a
smaller save allocation and is therefore a semantic rather than byte-for-byte
control for those files.

F100-baseline.mus
    sha256 9b6605da705949155d2690bc51680576b87872272406077fb59160fb599f00b9
    4,143 bytes. Unmodified new-document defaults.

F100-music-font.mus
    sha256 931417967ea03e945f532e4157fb6ed210aa3989e6848d9b3c17ad10b67edadc
    4,655 bytes. Changes the music default to Symbol at 60 points. The raw
    source tuple is `02(65534)` words 0-2: `(12, 60, 0)`.

F100-text-font.mus
    sha256 b393e9b2d8ef844f64967ae208ac50e452dc1672e87b8b569ff8c3cbea3e015c
    4,655 bytes. Changes the text-block default to New York at 17 points,
    bold and italic. The raw source tuple is `26(65534)` words 0-2:
    `(2, 17, 3)`.

F100-lyric-verse.mus
    sha256 012c07efcaadf2d17f31a9b4ce6ffe632c2edd34bc894d698a46f37c47994618
    4,655 bytes. Changes the lyric-verse default to Geneva at 13 points with

    `(3, 13, 28)`.
