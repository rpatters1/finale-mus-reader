F372-clef-baseline.mus / .etf
    sha256 recorded by the survey. Changes two clefs' baseline adjustments and
    toggles the "use baseline adjustments" checkbox on. Selector 28 word 4 moves
    -2 -> 1, selector 28 word 5 moves 0 -> 1, and selector 29 word 4 moves -4 -> -2.
    Finale 27 then converts every clef, including selector 30 which this save never
    touched, at 768 Efix per harmonic level: 768, -1536, -3840. This pair is what
    establishes both the conversion factor and the switch.
    Selectors 62 and 63 also move 415 -> 414, which is unrelated to clefs.
