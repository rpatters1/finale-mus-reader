Mac OS 9.0.4 running in SheepShaver

F2003-empty.mus and F2003-empty.etf
    Untouched new document used as the parent for the controlled bend-connection edits.

F2003-bendcnct-changed.mus and F2003-bendcnct-changed.etf
    Derived from F2003-empty. Distinct vertical-offset edits in Finale 2003's three visible bend
    connection controls change selector 98 tuple types 0, 1, 2, 3, and 6. The Finale 27 companion
    resets every edited value and also changes the hidden top-line types 4, 5, and 7 from 48 to 40.

F2003-bendcnct-change-x.mus and F2003-bendcnct-change-x.etf
    Derived from F2003-empty. Distinct horizontal-offset edits change the same five selector 98
    tuple types. The Finale 27 companion resets every edited value. Together with the vertical
    fixture, this establishes lossy upgrade behavior for Finale 2003 bend-connection offsets.
