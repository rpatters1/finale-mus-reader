Finale 2012 (internal version 17.0), zlib era, little-endian.

Origin: a test case authored for the Note Spacing plug-in and released for
publication by its author.

This file is a back-save. Finale 2014 through Finale 27 can write a musx document
out as a Finale 2012 MUS file, and that is what happened here, which is why the
name in the original collection refers to Finale 2014 while the banner reads
"Finale(R) 2012 File Converter". The two header tuples record the two roles: the
creator tuple keeps the authoring application, internal 18.0 for Finale 2014, and
the last-saver tuple records the format actually written, internal 17.0 for
Finale 2012. Reading the last-saver tuple therefore yields the version that
governs the file's layout, which is what the reader gates on.

    The tracked fixture for the 2007-2012 record encoding, which shares the
    logical model of class, comparator, and incidence with every earlier era but
    none of the physical one: records are class-identified and length-governed
    rather than fixed 16-byte rows. It carries sixteen font definitions and is
    the regression test for that separate decoding pathway.
